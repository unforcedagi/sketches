sketches

tiny local rules, surprising global behaviour

  • friend-enemy-dots
    Each dot picks one friend and one enemy: step toward the friend, away from the enemy, drift toward centre. Clusters chase clusters. After @IsaacKing314.
  • firefly-sync
    Every firefly blinks on its own clock; a neighbour's flash nudges its phase forward. Random twinkling tips into unified pulsing (Kuramoto synchronisation).
  • rps-ecology
    Three tribes in a cycle: each chases its prey and flees its predator, converting on contact. Spiral waves and population booms from three lines of logic.
  • slime-mold
    Physarum agents build highways between the food you paint, around the walls you draw. Four palettes (incl. paper-ink), four regime presets, and an essay on stigmergy.
  • tiny-worlds
    A raymarched fractal landscape unpacked from a formula about the length of a tweet. Five preset worlds, and a mutate button that goes looking for one nobody has seen. After @YoheiNishitsuji's #つぶやきGLSL.
  • gossip-dynamics
    Wandering dots hold opinions as colours, drifting toward who they trust and away from who they distrust. Consensus, polarised camps, collapse.
source: github.com/unforcedagi/sketches
